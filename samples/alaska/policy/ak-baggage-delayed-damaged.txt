Our 20-minute baggage guarantee
We know that instead of waiting at baggage claim you'd rather be out enjoying your destination, or settling in back at home. That's why we are committed to getting you on your way faster, and we've been making that commitment for years.
If your bags are not at baggage claim within 20 minutes of the arrival of your Alaska or Hawaiian Airlines flight at the gate, we’ll offer you a $25 discount code for use on a future Alaska or Hawaiian Airlines flight, or 2,500 Atmos™ Rewards bonus points.
In the rare instance that we don't meet the 20-minute guarantee, just get ahold of us at the baggage office within 2 hours of your flight's arrival for your discount code or points. You can simply speak with one of our agents at the airport.
