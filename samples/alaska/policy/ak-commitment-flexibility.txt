For travel more than 24 hours after booking (original purchase)
Whole, unused, non-refundable reservations (including Saver fares) canceled within 24 hours of original purchase may be refunded to the original form of payment, as long as travel commences more than 24 hours after booking.
Saver fares are eligible for a credit of 50% of the ticket value when canceled at least 14 days prior to departure of the first flight on the ticket.
