The following schedule changes qualify the guest for a full refund to original form of payment:
The cancellation of a scheduled flight where no Alaska flight of comparable routing is available within one hundred twenty (120) minutes of the original time of departure; or
A change in the scheduled departure time of an Alaska flight which exceeds one hundred twenty (120) minutes; or
A change in the scheduled departure time of an Alaska flight which exceeds one hundred twenty (120) minutes; or
A change in the routing of a scheduled Alaska flight which adds one (1) or more stops to the original itinerary; or
A change in the routing of an Alaska scheduled flight that results in a scheduled arrival time more than one hundred twenty (120) minutes later than the original scheduled arrival time; or
Any change in the arrival time of an Alaska flight that results in a misconnection to any flight shown in the same reservation and Ticket
